    THE SYSTEM RECORDS EVERYTHING YOU DO

    Every button, sixty times a second. That recording is what makes the seal worth anything — anyone can replay your run and watch you earn it. It is also how other crawlers race your ghost.

    A private run is still replayed, still certified, and still takes its place on the board. It is simply never handed to anyone else.
